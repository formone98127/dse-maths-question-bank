題目（英文卷）

11.   It is given that $p(x)$ partly varies as $x$ and partly varies as $x^{2}$. Suppose that $p(7)=56$ and $p(9)=216$.

(a) Find $p(x)$. (3 marks)

(b) Let $c$ be a real constant. Find the range of values of $c$ such that the equation $p(x)=c$ has two distinct real roots. (3 marks)
