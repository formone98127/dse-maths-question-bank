6. Let $\frac{\pi}{2} < x < \frac{3\pi}{4}$.

(a) Find a pair of constants $h$ and $k$ such that $\frac{\sec x + \tan x}{\csc x - \cot x} + \frac{\sec x - \tan x}{\csc x + \cot x} = h \sec x \csc x + k$.

(b) Solve the equation $\frac{\sec x + \tan x}{\csc x - \cot x} + \frac{\sec x - \tan x}{\csc x + \cot x} + 6 = 0$.
