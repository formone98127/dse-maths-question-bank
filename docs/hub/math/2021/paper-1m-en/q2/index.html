Question

The probability that a person has disease $D$ is 0.12. Test $T$ is used to show whether a person has disease $D$ or not. For a person who has disease $D$, the probability that test $T$ shows that the person has disease $D$ is 0.97. For a person who does not have disease $D$, the probability that test $T$ shows that the person does not have disease $D$ is 0.89.

(a) Find the probability that test $T$ shows a correct result.

(b) Find the probability that test $T$ shows that a person has disease $D$.

(c) Given that a person is shown to have disease $D$ by test $T$, is the probability that the person actually has disease $D$ less than 0.6? Explain your answer.

(6 marks)

深度分析

條件概率與貝氏定理　認知層次：分析　建議作答 180 秒

核心公式／定理：$P(A \cap B) = P(A|B)P(B)$、$P(A|B) = \frac{P(A \cap B)}{P(B)}$、$P(A \cup B) = P(A) + P(B) - P(A \cap B)$

提示 1（啟發）
先畫樹狀圖或文氏圖釐清事件關係。
提示 2（知識）
記住 P(正確) = 真陽性率×患病率 + 真陰性率×非患病率。
提示 3（破題）
貝氏定理的關鍵在於分母是總機率 P(T+)，而非單一條件機率。

速解策略：先算 P(正確)=0.97×0.12+0.89×0.88；再算 P(T+)=P(T+|D)P(D)+P(T+|非 D)P(非 D)；最後用貝氏定理求 P(D|T+) 並與 0.6 比較。
