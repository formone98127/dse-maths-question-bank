Question

10. An engineer models the rates of the production of an alloy in the first 10 weeks by two new machines $A$ and $B$ respectively by

$$\frac{dx}{dt}=\frac{6t}{(t+1)^{5/2}} \quad \text{and} \quad \frac{dy}{dt}=\frac{15\ln(t^2+100)}{16} \quad \text{for } 0 \le t \le 10,$$

where $x$ (in million kg) and $y$ (in million kg) are the amount of the alloy produced by machines $A$ and $B$ respectively, and $t$ (in weeks) is the time elapsed since the beginning of the production.

(a) Using the substitution $u = t+1$, find the amount of the alloy produced by machine $A$ in the first 10 weeks. (4 marks)

(b) Using the trapezoidal rule with 5 sub-intervals, estimate the amount of the alloy produced by machine $B$ in the first 10 weeks. (2 marks)

(c) The engineer uses the results of (a) and (b) to claim that machine $B$ is more productive than machine $A$ in the first 10 weeks. Do you agree? Explain your answer. (4 marks)

深度分析

積分法　認知層次：應用　建議作答 360 秒

核心公式／定理：\int \frac{6t}{(t+1)^{5/2}} dt、\text{Trapezoidal Rule: } \frac{h}{2}[y_0 + 2y_1 + ... + 2y_{n-1} + y_n]

提示 1（啟發）
Hint1: 代換法關鍵在於調整 dt 為 du。
提示 2（知識）
Hint2: 梯形法則 h = (b-a)/n，此處 n=5。
提示 3（破題）
Hint3: 比較時務必使用計算出的數值而非直覺。

速解策略：先代換積分 A，再列梯形公式算 B，最後比較數值下結論。注意對數計算與單位。
