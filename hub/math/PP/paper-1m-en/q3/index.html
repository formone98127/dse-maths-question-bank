Question

3. A glass container is in the shape of a vertically inverted right circular cone of base radius $15\text{ cm}$ and height $20\text{ cm}$. Initially, the container is full of water. Suppose the water is running out from it at a constant rate of $2\pi\text{ cm}^3/\text{s}$. Let $h\text{ cm}$ be the depth of water remaining in the container, $r\text{ cm}$ be the radius of the water surface (see Figure 1), $V\text{ cm}^3$ be the volume of the water, and $A\text{ cm}^2$ be the area of the wet surface of the container. It is given that $V=\dfrac{1}{3}\pi r^2 h$ and $A=\pi r\sqrt{r^2+h^2}$.

(a) Express $V$ and $A$ in terms of $r$ only.

(b) When $r=3$,

(i) find the rate of change of the radius of the water surface;

(ii) find the rate of change of the area of the wet surface of the container. (6 marks)

深度分析

微分法　認知層次：應用　建議作答 180 秒

核心公式／定理：$V = \frac{1}{3}\pi r^2 h$、$A = \pi r\sqrt{r^2+h^2}$、$\frac{dV}{dt} = -2\pi$

提示 1（啟發）
提示：利用倒立錐的相似三角形性質，找出 h 與 r 的線性關係。
提示 2（知識）
提示：記住體積公式 V 對 t 求導時需使用鏈式法則 dV/dt = (dV/dr) * (dr/dt)。
提示 3（破題）
提示：面積 A 包含根號項，微分時注意乘積法則與鏈式法則的組合應用。

速解策略：先由相似比得 h=5/3 r，代換 V, A 成單變量；再分別對 t 求導並代入數值計算。
