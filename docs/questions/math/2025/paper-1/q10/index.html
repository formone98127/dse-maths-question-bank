2025 DSE 數學 Paper 1 第 10 題 評卷參考＋詳解

題目（英文卷）

10.   Let $f(x)=2x^{3}+hx^{2}+kx+15$, where $h$ and $k$ are constants. When $f(x)$ is divided by $x+2$, the remainder is $-45$. It is given that $f(x)$ is divisible by $2x-5$.

(a) Find $h$ and $k$. (3 marks)

(b) Someone claims that all the roots of the equation $f(x)=0$ are rational numbers. Do you agree? Explain your answer. (3 marks)
