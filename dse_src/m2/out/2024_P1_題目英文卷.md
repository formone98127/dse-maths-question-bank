# 2024 DSE 數學 M2 Paper 1 — 題目（英文卷）

## 第 1 題

1. Let $O, A$ and $B$ be three distinct points in a rectangular coordinate plane such that $|\overrightarrow{OB}| = |\overrightarrow{OA}| + |\overrightarrow{AB}|$, where $O$ is the origin.
(a) Describe the geometric relationship between $O, A$ and $B$.
(b) Suppose that $\overrightarrow{OA} = 3\mathbf{i} + 4\mathbf{j}$ and $|\overrightarrow{OB}| = 20$. Find $\overrightarrow{AB}$.
(3 marks)

## 第 2 題

2. Let $x > 0$.
(a) Find $\lim_{h \to 0} \frac{\sqrt{x+h} - \sqrt{x}}{h}$.
(b) Find $\frac{d}{dx} e^{\sqrt{x}}$ from first principles.
(5 marks)

## 第 3 題

3. Let $m$ be a positive integer. Consider the expansion of $\left(x^m - \frac{2}{x}\right)^{24}$ in descending powers of $x$.
(a) Find the first 3 terms of the expansion.
(b) If the 19th term of the expansion is a constant, find $m$ and the coefficient of $x^{60}$ in the expansion. (5 marks)

## 第 4 題

4. (a) Let $0 < x < \frac{\pi}{2}$. Prove that $\csc 2x - \cot 2x = \tan x$.
(b) Solve the equation $(\csc 3\theta - \cot 3\theta)(\csc \theta - \cot \theta) = 1$, where $\frac{\pi}{6} < \theta < \frac{\pi}{3}$.
(5 marks)

## 第 5 題

5. (a) Let $k$ be a constant.
Using integration by parts, prove that $\int \cos(k \ln x) \mathrm{d}x = \frac{x}{1+k^2} \left( \cos(k \ln x) + k \sin(k \ln x) \right) + \text{constant}.$
(b) Using (a), or otherwise, evaluate $\int_1^e \sin^2(\pi \ln x) \mathrm{d}x$.
(6 marks)

## 第 6 題

6. Consider the system of linear equations in real variables $x, y, z$
$$E: \begin{cases} 3x + y - 9z = 0 \\ 2x + y - 7z = 0 \end{cases}$$
(a) Solve $(E)$.
(b) Someone claims that $(E)$ has a unique solution $(x, y, z)$ satisfying $\sin x + \cos y - \cos z = 0$, where $0 < z < \frac{\pi}{2}$. Do you agree? Explain your answer.
(6 marks)

## 第 7 題

7. Let $C$ be the curve $x^2y + 2xy^2 + 8 = 0$.
(a) Find $\frac{dy}{dx}$ in terms of $x$ and $y$.
(b) Someone claims that two of the tangents to $C$ are parallel to the straight line $x + 2y + 1 = 0$. Do you agree? Explain your answer.
(6 marks)

## 第 8 題

8. (a) Using mathematical induction, prove that $\sum_{r=1}^{n} r(2^{-r}) = 2 - (n+2)(2^{-n})$ for all positive integers $n$.
(b) Evaluate
(i) $\sum_{r=1}^{1999} r(2^{-r})$,
(ii) $\sum_{r=1}^{1000} (2000-r)2^{r}$.
(7 marks)

## 第 9 題

9. Two toy cars, $A$ and $B$, move along a straight line towards east with constant speeds 4 metres per second and 1 metre per second respectively. They start at the point $O$ at the same time. The fixed point $C$ is 10 metres due north of $O$. The following figure shows the positions of $A$ and $B$ after $t$ seconds. Let $\angle ACB = \theta$ radians.
(a) Prove that $\tan \theta = \frac{15t}{2(t^2 + 25)}$.
(b) It is given that $\angle BAC = \angle ACB$ when $t = T$. Find
(i) $T$,
(ii) the rate of change of $\theta$ when $t = T$.
(7 marks)

## 第 10 題

10. Define $f(x) = \frac{2(x+2)^2}{2x^2 + 6x + 5}$ for all $x \in \mathbb{R}$. Denote the graph of $y = f(x)$ by $G$.
(a) Find the asymptote(s) of $G$. (2 marks)
(b) Find $f'(x)$. (2 marks)
(c) Find the maximum point(s) and the minimum point(s) of $G$. (3 marks)
(d) Sketch $G$. (3 marks)
(e) Find the area of the region bounded by $G$, the $x$-axis and the $y$-axis. (3 marks)

## 第 11 題

11. (a) Let $a$ be a non-zero constant. Using integration by substitution, find $\int \frac{1}{x^2 + a^2} \, dx$ in terms of $a$.
(3 marks)
(b) Let $g(x)$ and $h(x)$ be continuous functions defined on $\mathbf{R}$. It is given that $g(x)$ is an even function and $h(x)$ is an odd function. Prove that $\int_{-c}^{c} \frac{g(x)}{1 + e^{h(x)}} \, dx = \int_{0}^{c} g(x) \, dx$, where $c$ is a constant.
(4 marks)
(c) Evaluate $\int_{-1}^{1} \frac{3^x + 3^{-x}}{(1 + e^{\sin^3 x})(9^x + 9^{-x} + 7)} \, dx$.
(5 marks)

## 第 12 題

12. (a) Consider the system of linear equations in real variables $x, y, z$
$$E: \begin{cases} 2x + z = -2 \\ -(2\lambda+5)x + \lambda y = -1, \text{ where } \lambda \in \mathbb{R}. \\ (\lambda+2)x + y - z = 0 \end{cases}$$
Assume that $(E)$ has a unique solution.
(i) Find the range of values of $\lambda$.
(ii) Solve $(E)$.
(6 marks)
(b) Let $u = hi + (2h+5)j - 2hk$ for any $h > 0$. Suppose that the vector $v$ is perpendicular to $(h+2)i + j - k$ and $u \times v = \mu i + 2hj - k$, where $\mu$ is a real number.
(i) Express $v$ and $\mu$ in terms of $h$.
(ii) Does there exist a value of $h$ such that the area of the parallelogram with adjacent sides $u$ and $v$ is 9? Explain your answer.
(7 marks)

## 第 13 題

13. Let $A = \begin{pmatrix} -2 & 2 \\ 1 & 2 \end{pmatrix}$ and $B = \begin{pmatrix} 4 & 2 \\ 1 & 5 \end{pmatrix}$.
(a) Evaluate $A - 6A^{-1}$ and $ABA^{-1}$. (4 marks)
(b) Find $B^n$, where $n$ is a positive integer. (3 marks)
(c) Does there exist a positive integer $k$ such that $A^{2k}B^{2k}(A^{-1})^{2k} = (ABA^{-1})^{2k}$? Explain your answer. (3 marks)
(d) Evaluate $A^{999}B^{999}(A^{-1})^{999}$. (2 marks)
