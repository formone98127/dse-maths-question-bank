Question

In a large farm, the weights of chickens follow a normal distribution with a mean of $\mu$ kg and a standard deviation of $\sigma$ kg. It is given that the percentage of chickens being lighter than 1.83 kg is the same as the percentage of those being heavier than 3.43 kg. Moreover, 89.04% chickens weigh between 1.83 kg and 3.43 kg.

(a) Find $\mu$ and $\sigma$.

(b) If 9 chickens are selected randomly from the farm, find the probability that the mean of their weights lies between 2.5 kg and 3.1 kg.

(5 marks)

深度分析

正態分佈　認知層次：應用　建議作答 180 秒

核心公式／定理：$Z = \frac{X - \mu}{\sigma}$、$P(a < \bar{X} < b) = P(\frac{a-\mu}{\sigma/\sqrt{n}} < Z < \frac{b-\mu}{\sigma/\sqrt{n}})$

提示 1（啟發）
Hint1: 正態曲線關於 \(\mu\) 對稱，兩尾機率相等意味著邊界中點即為 \(\mu\)。
提示 2（知識）
Hint2: 89.04% 對應於 \(-Z_{0.0598}\) 至 \(+Z_{0.0598}\)，查表可得 Z 值。
提示 3（破題）
Hint3: 樣本平均數分佈標準差為 \(\sigma/\sqrt{n}\)，切勿遺漏根號 n。

速解策略：先由對稱性得 \(\mu=2.63\)，再由面積得 \(Z\approx1.75\) 求 \(\sigma\)。第二部分用 \(\sigma/\sqrt{9}\) 標準化後查表。
