## 第 19 題

【建議標籤】

- 卷別/部分：Paper 1　乙部
- 課題：#二次函數

【考生表現】

Fair. Many candidates did not verify that f(3) = 44 , and hence were unable to prove that the graph of y = f(x) passes through F .Fair. Many candidates wrongly thought that g(x) = -f(x)+4 and were unable to use the method of completing the square to express the coordinates of U in terms of k .Poor. Most candidates overlooked that the area of the circle passing through F and O is the least when FO is a diameter of the circle, hence they were unable to find the value of k .Poor. Most candidates were unable to find the coordinates of G . Over 95% of the candidates were unable to explain why F , G , O and V are not concyclic

【題目內容】

Let \( f(x) = \frac{1}{1+k} \left( x^2 + (6k-2)x + (9k+25) \right) \), where \( k \) is a positive constant. Denote the point \( (4, 33) \) by \( F \).

(a) Prove that the graph of \( y = f(x) \) passes through \( F \). (1 mark)

(b) The graph of \( y = g(x) \) is obtained by reflecting the graph of \( y = f(x) \) with respect to the \( y \)-axis and then translating the resulting graph upwards by 4 units. Let \( U \) be the vertex of the graph of \( y = g(x) \). Denote the origin by \( O \).

(i) Using the method of completing the square, express the coordinates of \( U \) in terms of \( k \). (3 marks)

(ii) Find \( k \) such that the area of the circle passing through \( F \), \( O \) and \( U \) is the least. (3 marks)

(iii) For any positive constant \( k \), the graph of \( y = g(x) \) passes through the same point \( G \). Let \( V \) be the vertex of the graph of \( y = g(x) \) such that the area of the circle passing through \( F \), \( O \) and \( V \) is the least. Are \( F \), \( G \), \( O \) and \( V \) concyclic? Explain your answer. (5 marks)
