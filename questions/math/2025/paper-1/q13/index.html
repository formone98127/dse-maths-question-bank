2025 DSE 數學 P1 Q13 答案＋詳解

題目（英文卷）

13.   The straight line $L$ cuts the positive $x$-axis and the positive $y$-axis at the points $M$ and $N$ respectively. Let $P$ be a moving point in the rectangular coordinate plane such that $MP=NP$. Denote the locus of $P$ by $\Gamma$. It is given that the equation of $\Gamma$ is $3x-2y-30=0$.

(a) Describe the geometric relationship between $\Gamma$ and $MN$. (1 mark)

(b) Find the equation of $L$. (3 marks)

(c) Suppose that $\Gamma$ cuts $L$ and the $x$-axis at the points $Q$ and $R$ respectively. Find the area of the quadrilateral $ONQR$, where $O$ is the origin. (3 marks)
