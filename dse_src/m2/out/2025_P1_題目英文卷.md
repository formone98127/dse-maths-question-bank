## 第 1 題

1. Let $(1 + mx)(1 + 5x)^n = \sum_{k=0}^{n+1} a_k x^k$, where $m$ is a constant and $n$ is a positive integer. If $a_1 = 27$ and $a_2 = 285$, find $m$ and $n$.
(4 marks)

## 第 2 題

2. Let $f(x) = \frac{2x}{\tan x}$, where $0 < x < \frac{\pi}{2}$.
(a) Prove that $f\left(\frac{\pi}{4} + h\right) - f\left(\frac{\pi}{4}\right) = \frac{2h - 2h \tan h - \pi \tan h}{1 + \tan h}$.
(b) Find $f'\left(\frac{\pi}{4}\right)$ from first principles.
(5 marks)

## 第 3 題

3. (a) Find the constant $k$ such that $\frac{d}{dx} \ln \left( \sqrt{x^4+9} - x^2 \right) = \frac{kx}{\sqrt{x^4+9}}$.
(b) At any point $(x, y)$ on the curve $\Gamma$, the slope of the tangent to $\Gamma$ is $\frac{4x^3 - x}{\sqrt{x^4+9}}$. It is given that $\Gamma$ passes through the point $(2, 5)$. Does $\Gamma$ pass through the point $(-2, 5)$? Explain your answer.
(6 marks)

## 第 4 題

4. Let $C = \begin{pmatrix} k & 1 \\ -1 & 2 \end{pmatrix}$ and $D = \begin{pmatrix} 2 & k-3 \\ k+3 & -4 \end{pmatrix}$, where $k$ is a real number. Find $k$ such that $CD = DC$.
(3 marks)

## 第 5 題

5. (a) Using mathematical induction, prove that $\sum_{k=1}^{n} (-1)^k k^2(k^2 - 1) = \frac{(-1)^n(n-1)n(n+1)(n+2)}{2}$ for all positive integers $n$.
(b) Using (a), evaluate $\sum_{k=10}^{60} (-1)^k k^2(k^2 - 1)$.
(6 marks)

## 第 6 題

6. Let $\frac{\pi}{2} < x < \frac{3\pi}{4}$.
(a) Find a pair of constants $h$ and $k$ such that $\frac{\sec x + \tan x}{\csc x - \cot x} + \frac{\sec x - \tan x}{\csc x + \cot x} = h \sec x \csc x + k$.
(b) Solve the equation $\frac{\sec x + \tan x}{\csc x - \cot x} + \frac{\sec x - \tan x}{\csc x + \cot x} + 6 = 0$.
(5 marks)

## 第 7 題

7. Let $B = \begin{pmatrix} 4 & 3 \\ 0 & 1 \end{pmatrix}$ and $n$ be a positive integer. Define $A = B - I$, where $I$ is the $2 \times 2$ identity matrix.
(a) (i) Find $A^3$.
(ii) Write down $A^n$ in terms of $n$.
(b) Using the fact that $(I + A)^n = I + \sum_{k=1}^{n} C_k^n A^k$, express $B^n$ in terms of $n$.
(c) Does there exist a positive integer $m$ such that $A^m - B^m$ is singular? Explain your answer.
(6 marks)

## 第 8 題

8. Consider the parabola $C: y = x^2 + 4$. $C$ cuts the $y$-axis at the point $P$. $Q$ is a moving point on $C$ with $h$ as its $x$-coordinate, where $h > 0$. The vertical line which passes through $Q$ cuts the $x$-axis at the point $R$. Denote the origin by $O$. Let $A$ be the area of the quadrilateral $OPQR$.
(a) By expressing $A$ in terms of $h$, find $\frac{dA}{dh}$.
(b) Suppose that $h = 3 - 2e^{-t}$, where $t (t > 0)$ is the time measured in seconds. Someone claims that the rate of change of $A$ has only one maximum value. Do you agree? Explain your answer.
(7 marks)

## 第 9 題

9. (a) Using integration by substitution, prove that $\int \frac{1}{(1+x^2)^2} \, dx = \frac{1}{2} \tan^{-1} x + \frac{x}{2(1+x^2)} + \text{constant}$.
(b) Consider the curve $C: y = \frac{1}{1+3x^2}$. Let $R$ be the region bounded by $C$, the straight line $x = 1$ and the two axes. Find the volume of the solid of revolution generated by revolving $R$ about the $x$-axis.
(8 marks)

## 第 10 題

10. Define $f(x) = \frac{x^3 + 25x}{x^2 + 3}$ for all $x \in \mathbb{R}$. Denote the graph of $y = f(x)$ by $G$.
(a) Find the asymptote(s) of $G$. (2 marks)
(b) Is $f(x)$ an increasing function? Explain your answer. (2 marks)
(c) Explain why $G$ has rotational symmetry about the origin. (1 mark)
(d) Find the point(s) of inflexion of $G$. (4 marks)
(e) Let $L$ be the straight line which passes through all the points of inflexion of $G$. Find the area of the region bounded by $G$ and $L$. (3 marks)

## 第 11 題

11. (a) Let $m$ be a positive integer. Express $\int_{0}^{\pi} e^{-x} \sin 2mx \, dx$ in terms of $m$. (3 marks)
(b) (i) Evaluate $\int_{0}^{\pi} e^{-x} \sin 5x \cos 3x \, dx$.
(ii) Evaluate $\int_{0}^{2\pi} e^{-x} \sin 5x \cos 3x \, dx$.
(6 marks)
(c) Evaluate $\int_{e}^{\frac{\pi}{2}} \cos(\ln x^5) \sin(\ln x^3) \, dx$. (4 marks)

## 第 12 題

12. Consider the system of linear equations in real variables $x, y, z$
$$(E): \begin{cases} x - (a+1)y + 2z = 1 \\ 2x + ay + 7z = 2 \\ 4x + (a+6)y + (3a+14)z = b \end{cases}$$
(a) Assume that $(E)$ has a unique solution.
(i) Find the range of values of $a$.
(ii) Express $y$ in terms of $a$ and $b$.
(5 marks)
(b) Assume that $(E)$ has infinitely many solutions and $a > 0$.
(i) Solve $(E)$.
(ii) Let $k$ be a real constant. If all the solutions of $(E)$ satisfy $kx + y + 4kz^2 > 0$, find the range of values of $k$.
(7 marks)

## 第 13 題

13. The position vectors of the points $A, B$ and $C$ are $\mathbf{i}-2\mathbf{j}+3\mathbf{k}$, $-2\mathbf{i}+5\mathbf{j}+2\mathbf{k}$ and $2\mathbf{i}-\mathbf{k}$ respectively. Denote the plane containing $A, B$ and $C$ by $\Pi$. Let $E$ be the projection of the point $D(-2,-6,2)$ on $\Pi$. It is given that $EA$ produced meets $BC$ at the point $F$.
(a) Find $\overrightarrow{AB} \times \overrightarrow{AC}$. (3 marks)
(b) Find $\overrightarrow{ED}$ and $\overrightarrow{EA}$. (4 marks)
(c) If $BF:FC=1:t$, find $t$. (3 marks)
(d) Find the volume of the pyramid $ABDF$. (3 marks)
