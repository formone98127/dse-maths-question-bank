SECTION B (50 marks)

9. Consider the curve $\Gamma: y = \frac{1}{3}\sqrt{12 - x^2}$, where $0 < x < 2\sqrt{3}$. Denote the tangent to $\Gamma$ at $x = 3$ by $L$.

(a) Find the equation of $L$.

(3 marks)

(b) Let $C$ be the curve $y = \sqrt{4 - x^2}$, where $0 < x < 2$. It is given that $L$ is a tangent to $C$. Find

(i) the point(s) of contact of $L$ and $C$;

(ii) the point(s) of intersection of $C$ and $\Gamma$;

(iii) the area of the region bounded by $L$, $C$ and $\Gamma$.
