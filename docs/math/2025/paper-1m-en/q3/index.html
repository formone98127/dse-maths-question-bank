Question

3. The time spent by each visitor in a park follows a probability distribution with a mean of $\mu$ hours. A random sample of 49 visitors is drawn from the park. A constructed 95% confidence interval for $\mu$ using the sample mean and the sample standard deviation is $(4.362, 5.538)$.

(a) Find the sample standard deviation.

(b) Another random sample of 36 visitors is drawn from the park. The sample mean and the sample standard deviation of this sample are 5 hours and 1.8 hours respectively. The two samples are combined. Construct a 90% confidence interval for $\mu$ using the combined sample.

[Hint: The sample standard deviation is $\sqrt{\dfrac{1}{n-1}\left(\sum_{i=1}^{n} x_i^2 - n\overline{x}^2\right)}$.] (7 marks)

深度分析

置信區間　認知層次：應用　建議作答 480 秒

核心公式／定理：\bar{x} \pm t_{n-1, \alpha/2} \frac{s}{\sqrt{n}}、\bar{x}_{comb} = \frac{n_1\bar{x}_1 + n_2\bar{x}_2}{n_1+n_2}、s_{comb}^2 = \frac{(n_1-1)s_1^2 + (n_2-1)s_2^2}{n_1+n_2-2}

提示 1（啟發）
回憶置信區間公式：均值 ± 臨界值 × (標準差/根號n)
提示 2（知識）
合併方差公式為加權平均，注意自由度調整
提示 3（破題）
90% 置信區間對應雙尾機率 0.10

速解策略：先由 CI 寬度反推 s1，再算合併均值與方差，最後代入新樣本 n=80 計算新 CI。
