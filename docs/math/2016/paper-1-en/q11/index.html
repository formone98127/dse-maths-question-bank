題目

An inverted right circular conical vessel contains some milk. The vessel is held vertically. The depth of milk in the vessel is 12 cm. Peter then pours $444\pi\ \text{cm}^3$ of milk into the vessel without overflowing. He now finds that the depth of milk in the vessel is 16 cm.

(a) Express the final volume of milk in the vessel in terms of $\pi$. (3 marks)

(b) Peter claims that the final area of the wet curved surface of the vessel is at least $800\ \text{cm}^2$. Do you agree? Explain your answer. (3 marks)

深度分析

微分法　認知層次：應用　建議作答 420 秒

核心公式／定理：\frac{d}{dt} \ln(u) = \frac{u'}{u}、\int_a^b f(t) dt \approx \frac{h}{2}[y_0 + 2(y_1+y_2+\dots+y_{n-1}) + y_n]、\frac{d}{dt}(t^n) = nt^{n-1}

提示 1（啟發）
提示 1：梯形則公式為 \frac{h}{2}[y_0 + 2(y_1+\dots+y_{n-1}) + y_n]，此題 n=4，故有 5 個點。
提示 2（知識）
提示 2：求 A''(t) 時，先將 A'(t) = \frac{2t-8}{t^2-8t+95} 視為商數形式再求導。
提示 3（破題）
提示 3：B(t) 積分可令 u=t+3，則 t=u-3，轉化為 \int (u-1)/\sqrt{u} du 易於計算。

速解策略：先算出 A(t) 的 7 個點值代入梯形公式；接著用商法則求二階導數；B(t) 積分用換元法；最後比較絕對差值。
