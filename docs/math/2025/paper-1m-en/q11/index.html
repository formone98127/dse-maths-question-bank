Question

11. (a) Using the trapezoidal rule with 4 sub-intervals, estimate $\displaystyle\int_{2}^{6} \ln(5^t+1)\,dt$. (2 marks)

(b) Let $P(t)$ (in million) be the population of a city, where $t$ ($t \ge 0$) is the number of years elapsed since the start of the year 2025. It is given that $P'(t) = \dfrac{1}{5^t+1}$ and $P(0) = 0$.

(i) Using integration by substitution and $\dfrac{1}{u(u-1)} = \dfrac{1}{u-1} - \dfrac{1}{u}$, find $P'(t)$.

(ii) Using the result of (a), estimate the change in the population of the city from the start of the year 2027 to the end of the year 2030.

(iii) Does the change in the population of the city from the start of the year 2027 to the end of the year 2030 exceed 1.7 million? Explain your answer. (12 marks)

深度分析

積分法　認知層次：應用　建議作答 240 秒

核心公式／定理：\frac{1}{u(u-1)} = \frac{1}{u-1} - \frac{1}{u}、\int f(g(t))g'(t)dt = F(g(t)) + C

提示 1（啟發）
提示 1：梯形法公式為 $\frac{h}{2}[y_0 + 2y_1 + \dots + y_n]$，注意步長 h=1。
提示 2（知識）
提示 2：題目給出分解式 $\frac{1}{u(u-1)} = \frac{1}{u-1} - \frac{1}{u}$ 用於處理 P'(t) 的積分準備。
提示 3（破題）
提示 3：梯形法對凹向上曲線會低估，對凹向下則高估；ln(5^t+1) 的二階導數符號決定誤差方向。

速解策略：先算 (a) 梯形和（h=1, t=2..6），再確認時間區間對應 t 值，比較數值與誤差方向即可答 (iii)。
