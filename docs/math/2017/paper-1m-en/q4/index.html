Question

Susan plays a game. In each trial of the game, her probability of winning a doll is 0.6. Susan plays the game until she wins a doll.

(a) Find the probability that Susan wins a doll at the 4th trial in the game.

(b) If Susan cannot win a doll in $k$ trials, then the probability that she wins a doll within 10 trials in the game is greater than 0.95. Find the greatest value of $k$.

(c) In each trial of the game, Susan has to pay \$15\$. Find the expected amount of money she has to pay to win a doll in the game.

(7 marks)

深度分析

幾何分佈　認知層次：應用　建議作答 240 秒

核心公式／定理：$P(X=k) = (1-p)^{k-1}p$、$E[X] = \frac{1}{p}$

提示 1（啟發）
回想：連續失敗 k-1 次，然後第 k 次成功的機率結構是什麼？
提示 2（知識）
提示：幾何分佈的期望公式 E[X] = 1/p 可直接應用於計算總成本。
提示 3（破題）
破題：不等式部分建議先算「前 10 次全部失敗」的機率，再用 1 減去它。

速解策略：先寫下 P(X=k)=(0.4)^(k-1)*0.6；(b)用 1-(0.4)^k>0.95；(c)期望次數=1/0.6，再乘$15。
