2025 DSE 數學 P1 Q16 答案＋詳解

題目（英文卷）

16.   It is given that $$\begin{cases}\log_{3}x+\log_{3}y=9\\[2pt] \log_{x}81-\log_{y}9=1\end{cases},$$ where $0<x<y$.

(a) Let $u=\log_{3}y$. Prove that $u^{2}-3u-18=0$. (2 marks)

(b) Find $x$. (2 marks)

官方評卷參考（Marking Scheme）

SolutionMarksRemarks
Note that ${\log }_{3}x = 9 - u$ .Since $\log_{x}{81} - {\log }_{y}9 = 1$ , we have $\frac{{\log }_{3}{81}}{{\log }_{3}x} - \frac{{\log }_{3}9}{{\log }_{3}y} = 1$ .So, we have $\frac{4}{{\log }_{3}x} - \frac{2}{{\log }_{3}y} = 1$ .Therefore, we have $\frac{4}{9 - u} - \frac{2}{u} = 1$ .Hence, we have ${4u} - 2\left( {9 - u}\right) = u\left( {9 - u}\right)$ .Thus, we have ${u}^{2} - {3u} - {18} = 0$ .1M1----(2)
(b)By (a), we have $u = 6$ or $u = -3$ .So, we have $\left\{ \begin{array}{l} x = {27} \\ y = {729} \end{array}\right.$ or $\left\{ \begin{array}{l} x = {531441} \\ y = \frac{1}{27} \end{array}\right.$ .Since $x < y$ , we have $x = {27}$ .1M1A----(2)f.t.
SolutionMarksRemarks

考評報告

（a）Fair. Many candidates found difficulty in applying the properties of logarithm, and hence they were unable to prove that $u^2 - 3u - 18 = 0$. （b）Fair. Many candidates were not aware that 0 &lt; x &lt; y , and hence they did not reject the unsuitable value of x
