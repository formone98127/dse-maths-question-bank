題目（英文卷）

15.   There are $8$ white plates, $4$ red plates and $1$ black plate in a box. If $3$ plates are randomly drawn from the box at the same time, find (a) the probability that the colours of the $3$ plates drawn are the same; (2 marks) (b) the probability that the colours of the $3$ plates drawn are all different. (2 marks)

考評報告

（a）Very good. Over 70% of the candidates were able to find the probability that the colours of the 3 plates drawn are the same. （b）Good. Many candidates were able to find the probability that the colours of the 3 plates drawn are all different. Some candidates wrongly applied the concept of complementary events, and hence they were unable to find the required probability
