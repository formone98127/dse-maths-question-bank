2024 DSE 數學 P1 Q11 答案＋詳解

題目（中文卷）

下面的幹葉圖顯示一群研究員在某月內用於閱讀期刊的時數的分佈。 | 幹（十位） | 葉（個位） | | --- | --- | | 2 | 0 0 1 a a a 8 8 9 9 | | 3 | 0 0 2 3 4 4 4 7 9 | | 4 | 0 b |

該分佈的平均值為 30。 (a) 求 $a$ 及 $b$。（3 分） (b) 寫出該分佈的最小可取分佈域。（1 分） (c) 求該分佈的最大可取四分位數間距。（3 分）

English version（英文卷題目）

The stem-and-leaf diagram below shows the distribution of the numbers of hours spent on reading journals in a month by a group of researchers.

Stem (tens) Leaf (units)
2 0 0 1 a a a 8 8 9 9
3 0 0 2 3 4 4 7 9
4 0 b

The mean of the distribution is 30.

(a) Find ( a ) and ( b ). (3 marks)

(b) Write down the least possible range of the distribution. (1 mark)

(c) Find the greatest possible inter-quartile range of the distribution. (3 marks)

官方評卷參考（Marking Scheme）

考評報告

（a）Very good. Most candidates were able to use the mean of the distribution to find the values of a and b. （b）Good. About 65% of the candidates were able to write down the least possible range of the distribution. （c）Good. Many candidates were able to find the greatest possible inter-quartile range of the distribution. Some candidates wrongly gave the least possible inter-quartile range of the distribution as the answer
