Question

12. An engineer develops a software. Define $u = e^{6-2t}$, where $t$ ($t \ge 0$) is the number of months elapsed since the start of the development of the software. The engineer models the total number of bugs found $N$ in the software by $N = Ae^{-u}$, where $A$ is a positive constant.

(a) Find $\dfrac{du}{dt}$. Hence, express $\dfrac{dN}{dt}$ in terms of $u$.

(b) Find a polynomial $p(u)$ in $u$ such that $\dfrac{d^2N}{dt^2} = Np(u)$.

(c) It is given that $\dfrac{dN}{dt}$ attains its extreme value when $t = t_0$.

(i) Find $t_0$.

(ii) Determine whether the extreme value of $\dfrac{dN}{dt}$ is a maximum value or a minimum value. Explain your answer.

(d) Estimate the total number of bugs found, in terms of $A$, after a very long time.

深度分析

微分方程　認知層次：應用　建議作答 240 秒

核心公式／定理：\frac{d}{dt}(e^{kt}) = ke^{kt}、\frac{dN}{dt} = \frac{dN}{du} \cdot \frac{du}{dt}

提示 1（啟發）
先將 N 視為 u 的函數，再將 u 視為 t 的函數進行兩次微分。
提示 2（知識）
注意 e^{6-2t} 中指數部分的導數會產生一個係數 -2e^{6-2t}。
提示 3（破題）
極值判斷可透過觀察 dN/dt 在 t₀ 兩側的符號變化來決定。

速解策略：先求 du/dt，代入鏈式法則得 dN/dt；再對 t 微分一次整理出 p(u)；令一階導數為零解 t₀，最後取極限。
