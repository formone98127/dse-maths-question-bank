2025 DSE 數學 Paper 1 第 19 題 評卷參考＋詳解

題目（英文卷）

19.   The equation of the straight line $L$ is $4x-3y+83=0$. The equation of the circle $C$ is $x^{2}+y^{2}+ax-2y+b=0$, where $a$ and $b$ are positive constants. It is given that $C$ passes through the point $(-10,9)$ and $L$ is a tangent to $C$.

(a) Prove that the equation $25x^{2}+(9a+640)x+(90a+4\,924)=0$ has equal roots. Hence, find $a$ and $b$. (4 marks)

(b) The coordinates of the point $P$ are $(-20,1)$. Let $Q$ be a point lying below $L$ such that $PQ$ is tangent to $C$ and $PQ=25$. Denote the centre of $C$ by $I$.

(i) Find $\cos \angle IPQ$.

(ii) Find, in surd form, the distance between $I$ and $Q$.

(iii) Let $R$ be a point lying on $L$ such that $C$ is the inscribed circle of $\triangle PQR$. Denote the radius of the circumcircle of $\triangle PQR$ by $r$. Someone claims that $r>PQ$. Is the claim correct? Explain your answer. (8 marks)

官方評卷參考（Marking Scheme）

SolutionMarksRemarks
(a) Since (-10, 9) lies on C, we have $10^2 + 9^2 - 10a - 2(9) + b = 0$.Hence, we have $163 - 10a + b = 0$.Putting $y = \frac{4x + 83}{3}$ in $x^2 + y^2 + ax - 2y + b = 0$,we have $x^2 + \left(\frac{4x + 83}{3}\right)^2 + ax - 2\left(\frac{4x + 83}{3}\right) + b = 0$.Simplifying, we have $25x^2 + (9a + 640)x + (90a + 4924) = 0$.Since L is a tangent to C, the above equation has equal roots.Therefore, we have $(9a + 640)^2 - 4(25)(90a + 4924) = 0$.So, we have $9a^2 + 280a - 9200 = 0$.Since a >0 and b >0, we have a = 20 and b = 37.1M1A----(4)1M1Afor both correct
(b) (i) Since the coordinates of I are (-10, 1), IP is a horizontal line.Since P lies on L and Q lies below L, we have $\tan \angle IPQ = \frac{4}{3}$.Thus, we have $\cos \angle IPQ = \frac{3}{5}$.1M1A0.6
(ii) Note that IP = 10.By cosine formula, we have$IQ^2 = IP^2 + PQ^2 - 2(IP)(PQ)\cos \angle IPQ$$IQ^2 = 10^2 + 25^2 - 2(10)(25)\left(\frac{3}{5}\right)$$IQ^2 = 425$$IQ = 5\sqrt{17}$Thus, the required distance is $5\sqrt{17}$.1M1A
(iii) By sine formula, we have$\frac{\sin \angle IQP}{IP} = \frac{\sin \angle IPQ}{IQ}$$\frac{\sin \angle IQP}{10} = \frac{4}{25\sqrt{17}}$$\angle IQP \approx 22.83365418^\circ$ or $\angle IQP \approx 157.1663458^\circ$ (rejected )Let G be the circumcentre of $\triangle PQR$.Note that $\angle QGR = 360^\circ - 2(2\angle IPQ) \approx 147.4795905^\circ$.$\angle GQP$$= \frac{180^\circ - \angle QGR}{2} + 2\angle IQP$$\approx 61.92751306^\circ$1M1Mf.t.
Since $\cos \angle GQP = \frac{PQ}{2r}$, we have $r = \frac{PQ}{2\cos \angle GQP}$.Since $60^\circ < \angle GQP < 90^\circ$, we have $0 < 2\cos \angle GQP < 1$.Therefore, we have r > PQ .Thus, the claim is correct.1A----(8)f.t.
