Question

10. In city $H$, the water consumption (in $\mathrm{m}^3$) of each family in a certain month follows a normal distribution with a mean of $\mu\,\mathrm{m}^3$ and a standard deviation of $4\,\mathrm{m}^3$.

(a) A survey is conducted to estimate $\mu$.

(i) A random sample of 16 families is selected and their water consumptions (in $\mathrm{m}^3$) in that month are recorded as follows:

$$17 \quad 17 \quad 18 \quad 19 \quad 19 \quad 20 \quad 20 \quad 21 \quad 21 \quad 21 \quad 22 \quad 23 \quad 23 \quad 23 \quad 24 \quad 24$$

Find a 95% confidence interval for $\mu$.

(ii) Find the least sample size to be taken such that the width of a 99.5% confidence interval for $\mu$ is less than 3.

(b) Suppose that $\mu = 20$. If the water consumption of a family in that month lies between $18\,\mathrm{m}^3$ and $23\,\mathrm{m}^3$, the family is regarded as ordinary.

(i) Find the percentage of ordinary families in city $H$.

(ii) The families in city $H$ are randomly selected one by one and their water consumptions in that month are recorded. The recording stops when 3 ordinary families are found. Given that more than 6 families are selected in this recording process, find the probability that the water consumptions of exactly 9 families are recorded. (7 marks)

深度分析

正態分佈與置信區間　認知層次：應用　建議作答 180 秒

核心公式／定理：$\bar{X} \sim N(\mu, \frac{\sigma}{\sqrt{n}})$、$\text{CI}_{95\%}: \bar{x} \pm 1.96\frac{\sigma}{\sqrt{n}}$、$\text{Width} = 2z_{\alpha/2}\frac{\sigma}{\sqrt{n}} < W$

提示 1（啟發）
Hint1: 樣本平均數需手動加總除以 16。
提示 2（知識）
Hint2: 置信區間寬度公式為 $2 \times z \times \frac{\sigma}{\sqrt{n}}$。
提示 3（破題）
Hint3: 「超過 6 家」意味著失敗次數（非普通家庭）少於 4 次。

速解策略：先算樣本平均數，再用固定 z 值 (1.96/2.807) 套公式；第二部分用負二項累積機率計算。
