Question

Among the students sitting for a Mathematics test, 73% of them had revised before the test. For those who had revised, their scores are real numbers which can be modelled by $N(59, 10^2)$; and for those who had not revised, their scores are real numbers which can be modelled by $N(35.2, 12^2)$. Students who scored at least 43 passed the test.

(a) Find the probability that a randomly selected student passed the test.

(b) Given that a randomly selected student passed the test, find the probability that he had not revised before the test.

(c) Ten students are randomly selected among those who passed the test. Find the probability that exactly four of them had not revised before the test.

(7 marks)

深度分析

概率　認知層次：應用　建議作答 300 秒

核心公式／定理：$P(X \geq 43) = 1 - P(Z < \frac{43-\mu}{\sigma})$、$P(A|B) = \frac{P(B|A)P(A)}{P(B)}$、$X \sim B(n, p)$

提示 1（啟發）
先畫樹狀圖理清 Revise/NoRevise 與 Pass/Fail 的關係。
提示 2（知識）
正態分佈計算時務必標記清楚兩個不同的 (μ, σ)。
提示 3（破題）
第 (c) 問的 p 值必須來自第 (b) 問的結果，而非直覺猜測。

速解策略：先算兩組通過率 -> 全概率求總通過率 -> 貝氏定理求條件機率 -> 二項分佈代入計算。
