題目（英文卷）

4.   The coordinates of the point $A$ are $(4,-2)$. $A$ is rotated clockwise about the origin $O$ through $90^\circ$ to $B$.

(a) Write down the coordinates of $B$.

(b) Suppose that $B$ is translated upwards by $t$ units to the point $C$. If $A$, $O$ and $C$ are collinear, find $t$. (3 marks)

官方評卷參考（Marking Scheme）

SolutionMarksRemarks
(a) (-2,-4)1A
(b) The coordinates of C are (-2,t-4).
$\frac{t-4}{-2} = \frac{-2}{4}$ 1M
t-4=11A
t=5--------(3)
