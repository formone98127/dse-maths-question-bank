題目

14.   The height and the base radius of a solid metal right circular cone are $45\ \text{cm}$ and $24\ \text{cm}$ respectively. The circular cone is divided into a circular cone and a frustum $X$ by a plane parallel to its base. It is given that the height of $X$ is $30\ \text{cm}$.

(a) Express the volume of $X$ in terms of $\pi$. (3 marks)

(b) Express the total surface area of $X$ in terms of $\pi$. (3 marks) (c) $X$ is melted and recast into a solid cube. Does the total surface area of the cube exceed the total surface area of $X$? Explain your answer. (3 marks)

深度分析

圓錐與圓台　認知層次：應用　建議作答 480 秒

核心公式／定理：$V = \frac{1}{3}\pi r^2 h$、$A_{\text{total}} = \pi r l + \pi R L + \pi r^2$

提示 1（啟發）
Hint1: 相似比 $h_{small}/H = r/R$ 是關鍵第一步。
提示 2（知識）
Hint2: 圓台表面積 = 上底 + 下底 + 側面積，勿忘兩個圓。
提示 3（破題）
Hint3: 熔鑄問題核心在於體積守恆，邊長需開立方根。

速解策略：先求小圓錐半徑 $r=18$，再算圓台尺寸；表面積加三個部分；立方體邊長由體積反推比較即可。
