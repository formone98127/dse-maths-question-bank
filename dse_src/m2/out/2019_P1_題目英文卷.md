## 第 1 題

**Tags:** Differentiation

Let $f(x) = \frac{10x}{7 + 3x^2}$. Prove that $f(1 + h) - f(1) = \frac{4h - 3h^2}{10 + 6h + 3h^2}$. Hence, find $f'(1)$ from first principles.

(4 marks)

## 第 2 題

**Tags:** Probability, Matrices & determinants

Let $P(x) = \begin{vmatrix} x + \lambda & 1 & 2 \\ 0 & (x + \lambda)^2 & 3 \\ 4 & 5 & (x + \lambda)^3 \end{vmatrix}$, where $\lambda \in \mathbb{R}$. It is given that the coefficient of $x^3$ in the expansion of $P(x)$ is 160. Find

(a) $\lambda$,

(b) $P'(0)$.

(5 marks)

## 第 3 題

**Tags:** Differentiation

A researcher performs an experiment to study the rate of change of the volume of liquid $X$ in a vessel. The experiment lasts for 24 hours. At the start of the experiment, the vessel contains \$580 \text{ cm}^3$ of liquid $X$. The researcher finds that during the experiment, $\frac{\text{d}V}{\text{d}t} = -2t$, where $V \text{ cm}^3$ is the volume of liquid $X$ in the vessel and $t\$ is the number of hours elapsed since the start of the experiment.

(a) The researcher claims that the vessel contains some liquid $X$ at the end of the experiment. Is the claim correct? Explain your answer.

(b) It is given that $V = h^2 + 24h$, where $h \text{ cm}$ is the depth of liquid $X$ in the vessel. Find the value of $\frac{\text{d}h}{\text{d}t}$ when $t = 18$.

(6 marks)

## 第 4 題

**Tags:** Differentiation, Exponential & log functions

Define $g(x) = \frac{\ln x}{\sqrt{x}}$ for all $x \in (0, 99)$. Denote the graph of $y = g(x)$ by $G$.

(a) Prove that $G$ has only one maximum point.

(b) Let $R$ be the region bounded by $G$, the $x$-axis and the vertical line passing through the maximum point of $G$. Find the volume of the solid of revolution generated by revolving $R$ about the $x$-axis.

(6 marks)

## 第 5 題

**Tags:** Differentiation

(a) Using mathematical induction, prove that $\sum_{k=n}^{2n} \frac{1}{k(k+1)} = \frac{n+1}{n(2n+1)}$ for all positive integers $n$.

(b) Using (a), evaluate $\sum_{k=50}^{200} \frac{1}{k(k+1)}$.

(7 marks)

## 第 6 題

**Tags:** Differentiation

Consider the system of linear equations in real variables $x, y, z$

$$(E): \begin{cases} x - 2y - 2z = \beta \\ 5x + \alpha y + \alpha z = 5\beta, \text{ where } \alpha, \beta \in \mathbb{R}. \\ 7x + (\alpha - 3)y + (2\alpha + 1)z = 8\beta \end{cases}$$

(a) Assume that $(E)$ has a unique solution.

(i) Find the range of values of $\alpha$.

(ii) Express $y$ in terms of $\alpha$ and $\beta$.

(b) Assume that $\alpha = -4$. If $(E)$ is inconsistent, find the range of values of $\beta$.

(7 marks)

**Tags:** Integration, Trigonometric functions

(a) Using integration by parts, find $\int e^x \sin \pi x \, dx$.

(b) Using integration by substitution, evaluate $\int_0^3 e^{3-x} \sin \pi x \, dx$.

(7 marks)

## 第 8 題

**Tags:** Differentiation

Let $h(x)$ be a continuous function defined on $\mathbb{R}^+$, where $\mathbb{R}^+$ is the set of positive real numbers. It is given that $h'(x) = \frac{2x^2 - 7x + 8}{x}$ for all $x > 0$.

(a) Is $h(x)$ an increasing function? Explain your answer.

(b) Denote the curve $y = h(x)$ by $H$. It is given that $H$ passes through the point $(1, 3)$. Find

(i) the equation of $H$,

(ii) the point(s) of inflexion of $H$.

(8 marks)

## 第 9 題

**Tags:** Differentiation, Integration

Consider the curve $\Gamma: y = \frac{1}{3}\sqrt{12 - x^2}$, where \$0 < x < 2\sqrt{3}$. Denote the tangent to $\Gamma$ at $x = 3$ by $L\$.

(a) Find the equation of $L$.

(3 marks)

(b) Let $C$ be the curve $y = \sqrt{4 - x^2}$, where \$0 < x < 2$. It is given that $L$ is a tangent to $C\$. Find

(i) the point(s) of contact of $L$ and $C$;

(ii) the point(s) of intersection of $C$ and $\Gamma$;

(iii) the area of the region bounded by $L$, $C$ and $\Gamma$.

(9 marks)

## 第 10 題

**Tags:** Integration, Trigonometric functions

(a) Let \$0 \leq x \leq \frac{\pi}{4}$. Prove that $\frac{1}{2 + \cos 2x} = \frac{\sec^2 x}{2 + \sec^2 x}\$. (1 mark)

(b) Evaluate $\int_0^{\frac{\pi}{4}} \frac{1}{2 + \cos 2x} \, dx$. (3 marks)

(c) Let $f(x)$ be a continuous function defined on $\mathbb{R}$ such that $f(-x) = -f(x)$ for all $x \in \mathbb{R}$. Prove that $\int_{-a}^{a} f(x) \ln(1 + e^x) \, dx = \int_0^a x f(x) \, dx$ for any $a \in \mathbb{R}$. (4 marks)

(d) Evaluate $\int_{\frac{-\pi}{4}}^{\frac{\pi}{4}} \frac{\sin 2x}{(2 + \cos 2x)^2} \ln(1 + e^x) \, dx$. (5 marks)

## 第 11 題

**Tags:** Matrices & determinants

Let $M = \begin{pmatrix} 2 & 7 \\ -1 & -6 \end{pmatrix}$. Denote the \$2 \times 2$ identity matrix by $I\$.

(a) Find a pair of real numbers $a$ and $b$ such that $M^2 = aM + bI$. (3 marks)

(b) Prove that \$6M^n = (1 - (-5)^n)M + (5 + (-5)^n)I$ for all positive integers $n\$. (4 marks)

(c) Does there exist a pair of \$2 \times 2$ real matrices $A$ and $B$ such that $(M^n)^{-1} = A + \frac{1}{(-5)^n}B$ for all positive integers $n\$? Explain your answer. (5 marks)
