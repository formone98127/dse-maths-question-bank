Question

11. The accumulative rainfall of city M on a certain day increases at a rate of $P$ mm per hour. It is given that

$$P = a(-t^2+10t+8)e^{bt},$$

where $a$ and $b$ are constants, and $t$ ($0 \le t \le 4$) is the number of hours elapsed since 7 am on that day. It is found that $\ln\left(\dfrac{P}{-t^2+10t+8}\right)$ is a linear function of $t$, and the graph of this linear function passes through the point $(3,-0.1)$ and the intercept on the horizontal axis is 2.5.

(a) Express $\ln\left(\dfrac{P}{-t^2+10t+8}\right)$ as a linear function of $t$. (1 mark)

(b) Find the exact values of $a$ and $b$. (3 marks)

(c) Using the trapezoidal rule with 4 sub-intervals, estimate the accumulative rainfall of city M from 7 am to 11 am on that day. (2 marks)

(d) The accumulative rainfall of city N on the same day increases at a rate of $Q$ mm per hour. It is given that

$$Q = \frac{16(2t+5)e^{0.4t}}{4te^{0.4t}+3},$$

where $t$ ($0 \le t \le 4$) is the number of hours elapsed since 7 am on that day.

(i) Find $\displaystyle\int Q\,dt$. (2 marks)

(ii) Someone claims that the sum of the accumulative rainfalls of city M and city N from 7 am to 11 am on that day is greater than 160 mm. Do you agree? Explain your answer. (8 marks)

深度分析

對數與指數　認知層次：應用　建議作答 180 秒

核心公式／定理：$\ln(xy) = \ln x + \ln y$、$y = mx+c$ (直線方程)、$\int_a^b f(t) dt \approx \frac{h}{2}[(y_0+2y_1+2y_2+2y_3+y_4)]$

提示 1（啟發）
提示 1：回憶 $\ln(AB) = \ln A + \ln B$ 的性質來簡化對數式。
提示 2（知識）
提示 2：直線的水平軸截距代表當 $y=0$ 時的 $x$ 值，即 $c/m = -2.5$。
提示 3（破題）
提示 3：梯形法步長 $h = \frac{b-a}{n}$，此題 $n=4$，區間為 $[0,4]$。

速解策略：先化簡對數式得直線方程，代入點求參數；再用梯形公式計算積分；最後對 $Q$ 做部分分式後積分比較大小。
