Question

In a random sample of 120 swimmers in a certain beach, 75 of them are not satisfied with the water quality of the beach. Let $p$ be the population proportion of the swimmers in this beach who are not satisfied with the water quality of the beach. Find an approximate 90% confidence interval for $p$.

(4 marks)

深度分析

統計：置信區間　認知層次：應用　建議作答 180 秒

核心公式／定理：\hat{p} \pm Z_{\alpha/2} \sqrt{\frac{\hat{p}(1-\hat{p})}{n}}

提示 1（啟發）
先計算 \hat{p} = x/n
提示 2（知識）
記得標準誤公式需開根號
提示 3（破題）
90% 置信水準對應雙側尾機率各 5%，Z=1.645

速解策略：算出 \hat{p}=0.625，查 Z=1.645，套公式計算區間上下限。
