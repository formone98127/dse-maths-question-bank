Question

19.   The equation of the straight line $L$ is $4x-3y+83=0$. The equation of the circle $C$ is $x^{2}+y^{2}+ax-2y+b=0$, where $a$ and $b$ are positive constants. It is given that $C$ passes through the point $(-10,9)$ and $L$ is a tangent to $C$.

(a) Prove that the equation $25x^{2}+(9a+640)x+(90a+4\,924)=0$ has equal roots. Hence, find $a$ and $b$. (4 marks)

(b) The coordinates of the point $P$ are $(-20,1)$. Let $Q$ be a point lying below $L$ such that $PQ$ is tangent to $C$ and $PQ=25$. Denote the centre of $C$ by $I$.

(i) Find $\cos \angle IPQ$.

(ii) Find, in surd form, the distance between $I$ and $Q$.

(iii) Let $R$ be a point lying on $L$ such that $C$ is the inscribed circle of $\triangle PQR$. Denote the radius of the circumcircle of $\triangle PQR$ by $r$. Someone claims that $r>PQ$. Is the claim correct? Explain your answer. (8 marks)
