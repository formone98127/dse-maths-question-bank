Question

A tank is used for collecting rain water. During a certain shower, rain water flows into the tank for 7 minutes. Let $V \text{ m}^3$ be the volume of rain water in the tank. It is given that

$$\frac{dV}{dt} = \sqrt{t+1} \sqrt{3-\sqrt{t+1}} \quad (0 \leq t \leq 7),$$

where $t$ is the number of minutes elapsed since rain water starts flowing into the tank. The tank is empty at $t=0$ and the rate of change of the volume of rain water in the tank attains its maximum value when $t=T$.

(a) Find $T$. (4 marks)

(b) Find the exact value of $V$ when $t=T$. (5 marks)

(c) The tank is in the shape of an inverted right circular cone of height 1 m and base radius 6 m. The tank is held vertically. Let $h$ be the depth of rain water in the tank. Find

(i) the constant $Q$ such that $$\frac{dV}{dt} = Qh^2 \frac{dh}{dt},$$

(ii) $$\left. \frac{dh}{dt} \right|_{t=T}.$$ (5 marks)

深度分析

微分法　認知層次：應用　建議作答 480 秒

核心公式／定理：\frac{d}{dt}f(g(t)) = f'(g(t)) \cdot g'(t)、V = \frac{1}{3}\pi r^2 h

提示 1（啟發）
提示 1：先將 \frac{dV}{dt} 寫成 (t+1)^{1/2}(3-(t+1)^{1/2})^{1/2} 再求導。
提示 2（知識）
提示 2：積分時注意 \int \sqrt{u} du = \frac{2}{3}u^{3/2} + C，並利用 V(0)=0 定 C。
提示 3（破題）
提示 3：倒圓錐中 r/h = 6/1，故 V = \frac{1}{3}\pi (6h)^2 h = 12\pi h^3

速解策略：先求 \frac{dV}{dt} 的極大值點 T，再積分得 V(T)，最後用相似比換算 \frac{dh}{dt}。
