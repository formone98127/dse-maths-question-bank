Question

12. (a) Let $u = \dfrac{t^2-\sqrt{2}t+1}{t^2+\sqrt{2}t+1}$. Find a polynomial $p(t)$ such that $\dfrac{du}{dt} = \dfrac{p(t)}{(t^2+\sqrt{2}t+1)^2}$.

(b) Using integration by substitution and the identity $t^4+1 = (t^2+\sqrt{2}t+1)(t^2-\sqrt{2}t+1)$, find $\displaystyle\int \frac{t^2-1}{t^4+1}\,dt$.

(c) A researcher studies the weight of a substance in an experiment. Let $W(t)$ (in g) be the weight of the substance at time $t$, where $t \ge 0$ is the number of hours elapsed since the study begins. The researcher models the rate of change of the weight of the substance by

$$W'(t) = \frac{t^2-1}{t^4+1} + \left(\frac{1}{3}\right)^t$$

(i) Find the change in the weight of the substance from $t = 0$ to $t = 2$.

(ii) It is given that $W(0) = 5$. Estimate the weight of the substance after a very long time.

深度分析

積分法　認知層次：應用　建議作答 300 秒

核心公式／定理：\frac{d}{dt}\left(\frac{u}{v}\right) = \frac{u'v - uv'}{v^2}、\int f(g(t))g'(t)\,dt = \int f(u)\,du

提示 1（啟發）
提示 1：商法則導出的分子 \(p(t)\) 通常次數較低，注意 \(t^2+\sqrt{2}t+1\) 的導數。
提示 2（知識）
提示 2：觀察 \(t^4+1\) 分解後，兩部分分別對應 \(u\) 與 \(-u\) 的形式，可簡化積分。
提示 3（破題）
提示 3：長時間後指數項 \((1/3)^t\) 會消失，只餘下有理函數部分的定積分結果。

速解策略：先求導找 \(p(t)\)，再利用分解式將無理分數拆成兩部分換元，最後加總指數項積分值。
