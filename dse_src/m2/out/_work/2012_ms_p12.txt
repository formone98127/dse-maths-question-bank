The shaded area $= \int_0^1\left(\frac{5\sqrt{3}}{3}-\sqrt{4-x^2}-\frac{2\sqrt{3}}{3}x^{\frac{1}{2}}\right)dx$
For $\int_0^1\sqrt{4-x^2}dx$, let $x=2\sin\phi$.

$\int_0^1\sqrt{4-x^2}dx=\int_0^{\frac{\pi}{6}}\sqrt{4-4\sin^2\phi}2\cos\phi d\phi$

$= [2\phi+\sin 2\phi]_{\frac{\pi}{6}}^{\frac{\pi}{6}}$
$= \frac{\pi}{3}+\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$
