Question

7. A computer programme adjusts the length and the breadth of a rectangular digital picture, such that the length of its diagonal remains constant while its breadth decreases at the constant rate of $0.5\text{ cm s}^{-1}$. Initially, the length and the breadth of the picture are $20\text{ cm}$ and $15\text{ cm}$ respectively. Denote the breadth of the picture by $x\text{ cm}$. Find the rate of change of the area of the picture when $x = 7$. (4 marks)

Deep analysis (Chinese)

The full step-by-step analysis (scaffolding hints, wrong-option traps, speed strategy) is written in Chinese.

展開中文深度分析（Open detailed analysis）

深度分析

微分法　認知層次：應用　建議作答 180 秒

核心公式／定理：$\frac{dA}{dt} = \frac{dA}{dx} \cdot \frac{dx}{dt}$、$a^2 + b^2 = c^2$

提示 1（啟發）
回憶直角三角形勾股定理應用於長方對角線
提示 2（知識）
注意 breadth 減少意味著 dx/dt 為負值
提示 3（破題）
面積 A = l x，需將 l 視為 x 的函數

速解策略：先由對角線固定求出 k，再微分得 l dl/dt + x dx/dt = 0，代入 x=7 求 l 與 dl/dt，最後乘 l 得 dA/dt。
