Question

The straight lines $L_{1}$ and $L_{2}$ are perpendicular to each other. The $y$-intercept of $L_{1}$ is 3. It is given that $L_{1}$ and $L_{2}$ intersect at the point (2, 6). Let $R$ be the region (including the boundary) bounded by $L_{1}$, $L_{2}$ and the $x$-axis.

(a) It is given that $R$ represents the solution of a system of inequalities. Find the system of inequalities. (3 marks)

(b) Find the least value of $8x - 5y$, where $(x, y)$ is a point lying in $R$. (2 marks)

深度分析

直線方程與不等式　認知層次：應用　建議作答 180 秒

核心公式／定理：$y = mx + c$、$m_1 \times m_2 = -1$

提示 1（啟發）
Recall: y-intercept 3 means line passes through (0,3).
提示 2（知識）
Perpendicular lines have slopes m and -1/m.
提示 3（破題）
Region R is bounded by L1, L2, and the x-axis (y=0).

速解策略：先求 L1 斜率得方程，由垂直性得 L2，再聯立 x 軸 y=0 找頂點，代入比較最小值。
