Question

The number of commercial emails that John receives each hour follows a Poisson distribution with a mean of 1.3 per hour, while the number of non-commercial emails that he receives each hour follows a Poisson distribution with a mean of 0.9 per hour.

(a) Find the probability that the number of non-commercial emails that John receives in a certain hour is fewer than 3. (3 marks)

(b) Find the probability that the number of commercial emails that John receives in 6 hours is 5. (2 marks)

(c) Find the probability that the number of emails that John receives in a certain hour is 2. (3 marks)

(d) Given that the number of emails that John receives in a certain hour is 2, find the probability that both emails are non-commercial emails. (3 marks)

(e) Given that the number of emails that John receives in a certain hour is fewer than 3, find the probability that John does not receive commercial email in that hour. (3 marks)

深度分析

Poisson Distribution　認知層次：應用　建議作答 300 秒

核心公式／定理：$P(X=k) = \frac{e^{-\lambda}\lambda^k}{k!}$、$X+Y \sim Poisson(\lambda_1 + \lambda_2)$

提示 1（啟發）
Recall the formula for Poisson probability mass function.
提示 2（知識）
Remember that the sum of independent Poisson variables is also Poisson with parameter equal to the sum of parameters.
提示 3（破題）
Use conditional probability formula P(A|B) = P(A and B)/P(B).

速解策略：先寫下 X~Po(1.3), Y~Po(0.9)。注意 (b) 時間變長需乘 6，(c)(d)(e) 涉及總數需合併分佈。條件概率務必算對分母。
