Question

An engineer models the rates of change of the amount of oil produced (in hundred barrels per day) by oil companies $X$ and $Y$ respectively by

$$f(t) = \ln(e^t - t) \text{ and } g(t) = \frac{8t}{1+t},$$

where $t$ (\$2 \leq t \leq 12\$) is the time measured in days.

(a) Using the trapezoidal rule with 5 sub-intervals, estimate the total amount of oil produced by oil company $X$ from $t = 2$ to $t = 12$.

(3 marks)

(b) Determine whether the estimate in (a) is an over-estimate or an under-estimate. Explain your answer.

(3 marks)

(c) Find $\int \frac{t}{1+t} \, dt$.

(3 marks)

(d) The engineer claims that the total amount of oil produced by oil company $X$ from $t = 2$ to $t = 12$ is less than that of oil company $Y$. Do you agree? Explain your answer.

(3 marks)

深度分析

積分法　認知層次：應用　建議作答 300 秒

核心公式／定理：\int_a^b f(t) dt \approx \frac{h}{2}[f(t_0) + 2f(t_1) + ... + 2f(t_n) + f(t_n)]、\ln(e^t - t)、\int \frac{u}{u+c} du

提示 1（啟發）
提示 1：梯形法則步長 h = (b-a)/n，務必確認端點是否包含。
提示 2（知識）
提示 2：若 f''(t) > 0 (凹向上)，梯形面積會大於實際曲線面積 (Over-estimate)。
提示 3（破題）
提示 3：積分 \frac{t}{1+t} 可視為 \frac{(t+1)-1}{1+t} = 1 - \frac{1}{1+t}。

速解策略：先算梯形數值，再求 f''(t) 判斷凹凸性解釋誤差，最後積分比較兩者總量。
