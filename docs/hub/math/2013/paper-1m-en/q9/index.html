Question

The lifetime of a randomly selected LED bulb produced by a manufacturer is assumed to be normally distributed with mean $\mu$ hours and standard deviation 5000 hours. It is known that 96.41% of the bulbs will have a lifetime shorter than 39000 hours.

(a) Find the value of $\mu$.

(b) Suppose a random sample of 100 bulbs is drawn. Find the probability that the mean lifetime of the sample lies between 30200 hours and 30800 hours.

(c) The manufacturer wants to select another random sample of $n$ bulbs such that the probability that the mean lifetime of the sample exceeding 28500 hours is at least 0.985. Find the least value of $n$.

(7 marks)

深度分析

正態分佈　認知層次：應用　建議作答 300 秒

核心公式／定理：$Z = \frac{X - \mu}{\sigma}$、$\bar{X} \sim N(\mu, \frac{\sigma}{\sqrt{n}})$、$P(Z < z) = \Phi(z)$

提示 1（啟發）
Hint1: 回憶正態分佈曲線下面積為 0.9641 時對應的 Z 值大約是多少？
提示 2（知識）
Hint2: 樣本平均數的分佈標準差（Standard Error）公式是什麼？記得有根號 n。
提示 3（破題）
Hint3: 當機率大於 0.5 時，Z 值應為正；解不等式時注意不等號方向與取整規則。

速解策略：先由 (a) 定出 $\mu$；(b) 用 CLT 公式算標準誤後查表；(c) 設不等式，將機率轉為 Z 值，解出 n 並向上取整。
