## 第 13 題

【建議標籤】

- 卷別/部分：Paper 1　甲部(2)
- 課題：#多項式

【考生表現】

Most candidates overlooked that 0 is a root of the equation h(x)=0, and hence they were unable to find the number of rational roots of the equation

【題目內容】

Define \( g(x) = x^3 + 5x^2 - 12x - 1 \). Let \( h(x) = 3x^4 + ax^3 - 16x^2 + bx + c \), where \( a \), \( b \), and \( c \) are constants. When \( h(x) \) is divided by \( g(x) \), the quotient and the remainder are equal.

(a) Find the quotient when \( h(x) \) is divided by \( g(x) \). (3 marks)

(b) How many rational roots does the equation \( h(x) = 0 \) have? Explain your answer. (4 marks)
