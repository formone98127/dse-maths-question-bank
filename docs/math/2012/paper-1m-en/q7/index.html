Question

The number of goals scored in a randomly selected match by a football team follows a Poisson distribution with mean $\lambda$. The probability that the team scores no goals in a match is 0.1653.

(a) Find the value of $\lambda$ correct to 1 decimal place.

(b) Find the probability that the team scores less than 3 goals in a match.

(c) It is known that the numbers of goals scored by the team in any two matches are independent. Find the probability that the team totally scores less than 3 goals in two randomly selected matches.

(5 marks)

深度分析

泊松分佈　認知層次：應用　建議作答 240 秒

核心公式／定理：$P(X=k) = \frac{e^{-\lambda} \lambda^k}{k!}$、$P(X < k) = \sum_{i=0}^{k-1} P(X=i)$

提示 1（啟發）
Recall Poisson formula for k=0.
提示 2（知識）
Sum P(0)+P(1)+P(2) for part (b).
提示 3（破題）
List all pairs (x,y) where x+y<3.

速解策略：先由 e^-λ=0.1653 求 λ，再算單場 P(<3)，最後列舉兩場組合 (0,0),(0,1),(0,2),(1,0),(1,1),(2,0) 並加總。
