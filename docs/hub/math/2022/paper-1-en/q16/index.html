Question

Let ( g(x) = 3x^2 + 12kx + 16k^2 + 8 ), where ( k ) is a non-zero real constant.

(a) Using the method of completing the square, express, in terms of ( k ), the coordinates of the vertex of the graph of ( y = g(x) ). (2 marks)

(b) On the same rectangular coordinate system, denote the vertex of the graph of ( y = g(x) ) and the vertex of the graph of ( y = 2g(-x) ) by ( A ) and ( B ) respectively. Let ( M ) be a point lying on ( AB ) such that the area of ( \Delta OBM ) is the triple of the area of ( \Delta OAM ), where ( O ) is the origin. Express, in terms of ( k ), the coordinates of ( M ). (3 marks)

深度分析

二次函數　認知層次：應用　建議作答 300 秒

核心公式／定理：$y = a(x-h)^2 + k$ 頂點為 $(h, k)$、$\text{Area} = \frac{1}{2} \times \text{base} \times \text{height}$

提示 1（啟發）
先將 g(x) 配方成標準式找出頂點 A
提示 2（知識）
注意 y=2g(-x) 代表水平對稱後垂直拉伸 2 倍
提示 3（破題）
觀察 O, A, B 位置，利用 BM=3AM 直接定 M 為 AB 內分點

速解策略：配方得 A( -2k, 8), B(2k, 16)。由面積比推 BM:MA=3:1，用內分點公式求 M(-0.4k, 12)
