題目

  1. A right circular cylindrical container of base radius $8\text{ cm}$ and height $64\text{ cm}$ and an inverted right circular conical vessel of base radius $20\text{ cm}$ and height $60\text{ cm}$ are held vertically. The container is fully filled with water. The water in the container is now poured into the vessel.

(a) Find the volume of water in the vessel in terms of $\pi$ . (2 marks)

(b) Find the depth of water in the vessel. (4 marks)

(c) If a solid metal sphere of radius $14\text{ cm}$ is then put into the vessel and the sphere is totally immersed in the water, will the water overflow? Explain your answer. (3 marks)

深度分析

立體圖形　認知層次：應用　建議作答 300 秒

核心公式／定理：$V_{cylinder} = \pi r^2 h$、$V_{cone} = \frac{1}{3}\pi r^2 h$、$V_{sphere} = \frac{4}{3}\pi r^3$

提示 1（啟發）
提示：圓錐倒置時，越往上（靠近頂點）截面越小，需利用相似比 $\frac{r}{h} = \text{常數}$。
提示 2（知識）
知識：圓錐體積公式為 $\frac{1}{3}\pi r^2 h$，注意係數 $\frac{1}{3}$。
提示 3（破題）
破題：先算出水的總量，再反推水深，最後比較球體與剩餘空間大小。

速解策略：先算圓柱水體積，再用相似比設水面半徑 $r$ 與水深 $h$ 關係，代入圓錐公式解 $h$；球體體積與剩餘空間比較即可。
