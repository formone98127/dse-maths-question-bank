題目

The bar chart below shows the distribution of the most favourite fruits of the students in a group. It is given that each student has only one most favourite fruit.

If a student is randomly selected from the group, then the probability that the most favourite fruit is apple is $\dfrac{3}{20}$.

(a) Find $k$. (3 marks)

(b) Suppose that the above distribution is represented by a pie chart.

(i) Find the angle of the sector representing that the most favourite fruit is orange.

(ii) Some new students now join the group and the most favourite fruit of each of these students is orange. Will the angle of the sector representing that the most favourite fruit is orange be doubled? Explain your answer. (4 marks)

深度分析

正態分佈　認知層次：應用　建議作答 300 秒

核心公式／定理：$Z = \frac{X - \mu}{\sigma}$、$P(X > x) = 1 - P(Z < z)$、$B(n, p)$、$\bar{X} \sim N(\mu, \frac{\sigma^2}{n})$

提示 1（啟發）
先畫出正態曲線圖，標記 k 的位置以理解左尾與右尾的百分比關係。
提示 2（知識）
二項分佈計算時，「多於 10」包含 11 和 12，切勿只算 P(X=11)。
提示 3（破題）
樣本平均數的標準差是總體標準差除以根號 n，這是易錯點。

速解策略：先求 p=P(T>6)，再套二項公式；利用正態曲線對稱性由百分位反推 k 值。
