Question

Mary conducts a survey to estimate the proportion $p$ of children in a city who learn recorder. In a random sample of 40 children from the city, 28 of them learn recorder.

(a) (i) Find the sample proportion of children who learn recorder.

(ii) Find an approximate 90% confidence interval for $p$.

(b) Mary now wants to construct an approximate 99% confidence interval for $p$ such that the width of the confidence interval does not exceed 0.1. Using the result of (a)(i), estimate the least number of children that Mary should survey.

(6 marks)

深度分析

統計　認知層次：應用　建議作答 300 秒

核心公式／定理：$\hat{p} = \frac{x}{n}$、$\hat{p} \pm z_{\alpha/2}\sqrt{\frac{\hat{p}(1-\hat{p})}{n}}$、$n = \left( \frac{z_{\alpha/2}}{E} \right)^2 \hat{p}(1-\hat{p})$

提示 1（啟發）
Hint1: 先算出 \hat{p}=0.7，這是後續所有計算的基礎參數。
提示 2（知識）
Hint2: 記住寬度 (Width) = 2 \times \text{Margin of Error}，題目給的是總寬度限制。
提示 3（破題）
Hint3: 求樣本量 n 時，公式中的 z 值要根據新的置信水平 (99%) 調整。

速解策略：先算 \hat{p}=0.7，再用 $n = \frac{z^2 \cdot 0.7 \cdot 0.3}{(0.1/2)^2}$ 代入 z=2.576 計算並向上取整。
