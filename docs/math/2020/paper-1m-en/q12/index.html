Question

12. John studies the number of ducks $P$ (in thousand) in a farm by

$$P = \frac{32}{a^{5+bt}+8}$$

where $a$ and $b$ are constants and $t$ ($t \ge 0$) is the number of months elapsed since the start of the study.

(a) Express $\ln\left(\dfrac{32}{P}-8\right)$ as a linear function of $t$.

(b) John finds that the graph of the linear function obtained in (a) passes through the point $(1, \ln 2)$ and the intercept on the vertical axis of the graph is $\ln 32$.

(i) Find $a$ and $b$.

(ii) Find $\dfrac{dP}{dt}$ and $\dfrac{d^2P}{dt^2}$.

(iii) Estimate the number of ducks in the farm after a very long time. Hence, or otherwise, prove that the number of ducks in the farm does not exceed 4 thousand since the start of the study.

(iv) Find the number of ducks in the farm when $\dfrac{dP}{dt}$ attains its greatest value. (11 marks)

深度分析

對數與微分　認知層次：分析　建議作答 420 秒

核心公式／定理：$\ln(x^k) = k\ln x$、$\frac{d}{dt}(e^{kt}) = ke^{kt}$、$\frac{d}{dx}(x^n) = nx^{n-1}$

提示 1（啟發）
Hint1: 先將 $P$ 的表達式變形為 $\frac{32}{P}-8 = a^5 \cdot (a^b)^t$ 的形式，再取對數。
提示 2（知識）
Hint2: 垂直軸截距即 $t=0$ 時的函數值，直接代入原式求 $a$。
提示 3（破題）
Hint3: 導數最大時，二階導數為零且三階導數小於零（或觀察一階導數的極大點）

速解策略：先化簡對數項得直線方程 $y = bt + \ln a^5$，代入兩點求參數；再直接對原式微分，令二階導為零找一階導最大值。
