Question

4. In each month, the probability that a store offers a discount to its products is $0.35$. If a discount is offered in a certain month, then the probability that the store makes a profit in that month is $0.7$; otherwise, the probability of making a profit in that month is $0.28$.

(a) Find the probability that the store makes a profit in a certain month.

(b) Given that the store makes a profit in a certain month, find the probability that the store offers a discount in that month.

(c) Find the probability that the store makes a profit in at least 2 months out of 12 months. (6 marks)

深度分析

條件概率與二項分佈　認知層次：應用　建議作答 180 秒

核心公式／定理：$P(A) = P(A|B)P(B) + P(A|B^c)P(B^c)$、$P(B|A) = \frac{P(A|B)P(B)}{P(A)}$、$\sum_{k=r}^{n} \binom{n}{k} p^k q^{n-k}$

提示 1（啟發）
先畫樹狀圖理清折扣與獲利兩種路徑。
提示 2（知識）
記住公式：P(折扣|獲利) = P(獲利|折扣)P(折扣) / P(獲利)。
提示 3（破題）
最後一步是二項分佈累積概率，注意題目是「至少」而非「恰好」。

速解策略：先算單月獲利機率 0.65，再用二項式求和 k=2..12。
