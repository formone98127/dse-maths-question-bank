Question

The polynomial $p(x)$ is divisible by $x-5$. When $p(x)$ is divided by $x^2+x+1$, the quotient and the remainder are $2x^2-37$ and $cx+c-1$ respectively, where $c$ is a constant.

(a) Find $c$. (3 marks)

(b) Prove that $x+3$ is a factor of $p(x)$. (1 mark)

(c) Someone claims that all the roots of the equation $p(x)=0$ are real numbers. Is the claim correct? Explain your answer. (3 marks)

深度分析

多項式　認知層次：分析　建議作答 420 秒

核心公式／定理：$p(x) = (x^2+x+1)(2x^2-37) + (cx+c-1)$、$p(5)=0$、$p(-3)=0$

提示 1（啟發）
Recall the division algorithm: Dividend = Divisor × Quotient + Remainder.
提示 2（知識）
Use the Factor Theorem: if $(x-a)$ is a factor, then $p(a)=0$.
提示 3（破題）
Check the discriminant of the quadratic factor to determine root nature.

速解策略：Write $p(x) = (x^2+x+1)(2x^2-37) + cx+c-1$. Set $x=5$ to find $c$, then substitute back and check $x=-3$.
