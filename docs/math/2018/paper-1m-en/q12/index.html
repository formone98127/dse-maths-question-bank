Question

In an experiment, the number of certain bacteria in a room under controlled conditions is recorded. The temperature $Q$ (in °C) in the room can be modelled by the following linear function

$$Q = \ln r + (s \ln 3)t,$$

where $r$ and $s$ are constants and $t$ (\$0 \leq t \leq 20$) is the number of hours elapsed since the start of the experiment. It is given that the slope and the intercept on the vertical axis of the graph of this linear function of $t$ are $-0.1 \ln 9$ and $\ln 9\$ respectively.

(a) Find $r$ and $s$.

(b) It is given that

$$Q = \ln \left( \frac{120 - 3N}{N} \right),$$

where $N$ is the number in millions of bacteria.

(i) Prove that $N = \frac{40}{3^{1-0.2t} + 1}$.

(ii) Is it possible that there are 4 million bacteria in the room during the experiment? Explain your answer.

(iii) Find $\frac{dN}{dt}$ and $\frac{d^2N}{dt^2}$.

(iv) Describe how $\frac{dN}{dt}$ varies during the experiment. Explain your answer.

(11 marks)

核心公式／定理：\frac{d}{dt}(e^{kt}) = ke^{kt}、\ln(a/b) = \ln a - \ln b

提示 1（啟發）
Hint1: 直線方程 y = mx + c，注意題目給的是 Q(t) 而非標準形式，需整理成 mt+c。
提示 2（知識）
Hint2: 利用 \ln(a/b) = \ln a - \ln b 將 Q 的兩個表達式聯立，解出 N。
提示 3（破題）
Hint3: dN/dt 的正負取決於二階導數的符號，觀察分母大小變化即可判斷趨勢。

速解策略：先由線性條件定 r,s；再代數操作得 N；接著微分求速率與加速度；最後根據二階導數符號描述變化率遞減。
