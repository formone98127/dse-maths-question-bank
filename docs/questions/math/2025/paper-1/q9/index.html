9.   The table below shows the distribution of the numbers of pens owned by some students.

Number of pens56789
Number of students9132$s$11

The median of the distribution is $7$.

(a) Find the least possible value of $s$.

(b) Write down the greatest possible value of $s$.

(c) Find the greatest possible standard deviation of the distribution. (5 marks)

官方評卷參考（Marking Scheme）

SolutionMarksRemarks
Let m be the least possible value of s .9 + 13 = 1 + m + 11m = 10Thus, the least possible value of s is 10 .1M1A1A
(b)12
(c)There are three cases.Case 1: s = 10The standard deviation of the distribution≈1.5127442161M----any one
Case 2: s = 11The standard deviation of the distribution≈1.502990088
