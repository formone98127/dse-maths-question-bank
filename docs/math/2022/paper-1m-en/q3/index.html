Question

3. Let $A$ and $B$ be two events. Denote the complementary events of $A$ and $B$ by $A'$ and $B'$ respectively. It is given that $\mathrm{P}(A' \mid B) = 5\mathrm{P}(A \mid B)$ and $\mathrm{P}(A \cap B') = \mathrm{P}(A \cap B) + 0.45$. Suppose that $\mathrm{P}(B) = p$, where $p \neq 0$.

(a) Prove that $\mathrm{P}(A) = \dfrac{p}{3} + 0.45$.

(b) Are $A$ and $B$ independent? Explain your answer.

(c) Let $C$ be an event such that $\mathrm{P}(C) = 0.6$. Are $A$ and $C$ mutually exclusive? Explain your answer. (7 marks)

深度分析

概率　認知層次：應用　建議作答 300 秒

核心公式／定理：$\mathrm{P}(A' \mid B) = 1 - \mathrm{P}(A \mid B)$、$\mathrm{P}(A \cap B') = \mathrm{P}(B')\mathrm{P}(A \mid B')$、獨立事件定義：$\mathrm{P}(A \cap C) = \mathrm{P}(A)\mathrm{P}(C)$

提示 1（啟發）
Hint1: 回憶 $\mathrm{P}(A' \mid B)$ 與 $\mathrm{P}(A \mid B)$ 的互補關係。
提示 2（知識）
Hint2: 獨立事件需滿足 $\mathrm{P}(A \cap B) = \mathrm{P}(A)\mathrm{P}(B)$，互斥則需 $\mathrm{P}(A \cap C)=0$。
提示 3（破題）
Hint3: 注意 (c) 中未給定 $A$ 與 $C$ 的交集資訊，思考能否從已知推導出矛盾或確定值。

速解策略：先由條件概率關係求 $\mathrm{P}(A \mid B)$，再代入交集方程解 $\mathrm{P}(A)$；獨立性比較兩邊概率，互斥性檢查交集是否為零。
