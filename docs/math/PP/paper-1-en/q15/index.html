Question

The mean score of a class of students in a test is $48$ marks. The scores of Mary and John in the test are $36$ marks and $66$ marks respectively. The standard score of Mary in the test is $-2$.

(a) Find the standard score of John in the test. (2 marks)

(b) A student, David, withdraws from the class and his test score is then deleted. It is given that his test score is $48$ marks. Will there be any change in the standard score of John due to the deletion of the test score of David? Explain your answer. (2 marks)

深度分析

統計　認知層次：應用　建議作答 120 秒

核心公式／定理：$z = \frac{x - \mu}{\sigma}$、$\text{新}z = z_{\text{舊}} + \frac{\Delta x - \Delta \mu}{\sigma_{\text{新}}}$

提示 1（啟發）
先由 Mary 的 $z$ 值反推 $\sigma$。
提示 2（知識）
David 分數等於原平均數，移除後新平均數仍為 $48$。
提示 3（破題）
但樣本量減少，離差平方和變化會導致標準差改變。

速解策略：先算 $\sigma=12$ 得 John $z=0.5$；David 移除後 $\mu$ 不變但 $\sigma$ 變大，故 John $z$ 值下降。
