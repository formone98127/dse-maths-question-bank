Question

17.   Let $T(n)$ be the $n$th term of an arithmetic sequence such that $T(1)\neq T(2)$ and $T(47)=456$. It is given that $T(9)$, $T(47)$, $T(199)$ is a geometric sequence.

(a) Find $T(1)$. (3 marks)

(b) Find the least value of $n$ such that the sum of the first $n$ terms of the arithmetic sequence is greater than $10^{6}$. (3 marks)

深度分析

數列　認知層次：分析　建議作答 300 秒

核心公式／定理：$T_n = T_1 + (n-1)d$、$S_n = \frac{n}{2}[2a+(n-1)d]$、$\frac{T_{47}}{T_9} = \left(\frac{T_{199}}{T_{47}}\right)$

提示 1（啟發）
Recall: T_n = a + (n-1)d and use the geometric mean property T_47^2 = T_9 * T_199.
提示 2（知識）
Substitute T_47=456 into the equation to solve for d first.
提示 3（破題）
For part (b), ensure S_n > 10^6 leads to a quadratic inequality in n.

速解策略：Use T_47=456 and geometric property to find d, then get a. For S_n>10^6, solve the resulting quadratic inequality for minimum integer n.
